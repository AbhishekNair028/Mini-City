❓ Controls
  • Touch: 1 finger builds (drag to paint). 2 fingers move and pinch-zoom. Press ✋ to move the map with 1 finger.
  • On-screen pad: hold ▲◀▶▼ to move, 🎯 to centre, ＋ － to zoom, ↶ to undo.
  • Mouse: left-click/drag builds, right-drag or Shift+drag moves, wheel zooms.
  • Keys: WASD/arrows move · + − zoom · Space pause/speed · Z undo · Q move mode · B bulldoze · [ ] cycle tools · 1-9 quick tools · O coverage · C centre · Esc cancel · H help
  • Minimap: tap it to jump anywhere.
